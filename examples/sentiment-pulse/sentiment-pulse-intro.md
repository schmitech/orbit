💬 **Sentiment Pulse: real-time sentiment analysis.** Paste a review or message and ORBIT's decision model scores it in a fraction of a second, returning typed answers instead of a chat reply: overall **polarity** (positive, negative, neutral or mixed), a signed **valence** scale, whether it's **sarcastic**, whether it **needs escalation**, and a stance on **price, support, product and delivery** — each with its probability.

**Try pasting texts like:**
- 🙂 "Does what it says, happy with it so far."
- 😡 "Cancel my account, this is ridiculous."
- 🙃 "Great, another update that broke login."
- 📦 "Arrived two days late but support fixed it in minutes. Love it."
- 📄 "Order #4471 shipped on the 3rd."
- 🤔 "Pretty disappointed, the hinge already feels loose after a week." (a borderline one: watch the confidence)

Note: this is a demo adapter and not yet enabled for general use — see `docs/roadmap/complete/decision-model-sentiment-analysis.md` for the evaluation plan it's waiting on.
