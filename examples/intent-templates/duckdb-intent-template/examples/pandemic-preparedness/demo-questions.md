# Demo questions: Canadian Pandemic Preparedness Demo

Sample natural-language questions to try against the `intent-duckdb-pandemic-preparedness`
adapter. All data is synthetic — built as an ORBIT showcase for a meeting with the
Canadian Pandemic Preparedness Hub (CP2H, University of Ottawa / McMaster University).

## Overview / counts
- How many biomanufacturing facilities do we have?
- How many clinical trials are active right now?
- Give me an overview of pandemic preparedness readiness

## Facility breakdowns
- Show me facilities by province
- Which provinces have the most biomanufacturing facilities?
- Show me the breakdown of facilities by type
- What's our total biomanufacturing capacity by province?
- Which province has the most production capacity?

## Facility filtering
- Show me GMP-certified operational facilities
- Show me facilities in Ontario
- What biomanufacturing facilities are in Quebec?

## Trial breakdowns
- Show me trials by phase
- How many candidates have reached Phase III?
- Show me trials by pathogen
- Which pathogens have the most clinical trials?

## Trial status
- Show me trials by status
- How many trials have been terminated?

## Trial filtering
- What clinical trials are in Phase III for coronavirus?
- Show me Phase II influenza trials
- Which RSV candidates have reached Phase III?
- Show me active clinical trials

## Facility ↔ trial joins
- What clinical trials are running at the McMaster facility?
- Which facilities are hosting mpox vaccine trials?
- What facilities support coronavirus research?
