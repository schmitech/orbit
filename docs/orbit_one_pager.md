# ORBIT

**Put AI to work with your information. Keep control of how it’s used.**

ORBIT is an open-source platform that connects AI to your organization’s documents, databases, and business tools. It helps you build assistants that answer questions using your information and carry out tasks through connected tools—with choices over AI providers, deployment, access, and usage limits.

People can use it through a chat or voice assistant, or through applications your organization already builds.

## What can you do with it?

| Everyday need | How ORBIT helps |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Find answers in company information** | Ask “What does our policy say?” and retrieve relevant information from connected documents, including PDFs, spreadsheets, and scanned files. |
| **Ask questions about business data** | Ask “What were sales by region last month?” and retrieve database results through configured query templates, with read-only safeguards for supported database queries. |
| **Help customers and employees** | Build chat or voice assistants that use your organization’s information to answer questions. |
| **Connect answers to actions** | Let assistants use connected business tools to complete tasks across multiple steps, within the permissions you configure. |

## Why it matters

- **Make information easier to use.** Bring documents, business data, and tools into a conversational experience, reducing the need to search across separate systems.
- **Choose where your AI runs.** Deploy on your own infrastructure or in a private cloud. Fully offline operation is possible with local models and compatible local services.
- **Keep your choice of AI providers.** Connect to cloud or locally hosted models and select different models for different tasks as your needs change.
- **Manage access and usage.** Configure sign-in, user permissions, usage limits, and dashboards showing activity and estimated AI costs.
- **Build on an open foundation.** ORBIT’s Apache 2.0 distribution includes its security and administration features without a separate paid feature tier. Hosting, model services, and operations still carry costs.

## Stay in control of your data and AI

ORBIT provides access controls, document encryption, activity logs, and optional checks for sensitive information and unsafe content. These help your team apply its policies and investigate how AI is being used.

Protection depends on the controls and services you enable. ORBIT supports security and compliance assessments; it does not automatically certify a deployment or guarantee correct AI answers. Your organization remains responsible for operating the system and reviewing consequential outputs.
